## 1. Dice (pp. 46–50)

- **Action roll:** two d12 Duality Dice (Hope die, Fear die) + trait
  modifier + other modifiers vs a Difficulty.
  - Meets Difficulty and Hope > Fear: success **with Hope** (gain 1 Hope).
  - Meets and Fear > Hope: success **with Fear** (GM gains 1 Fear).
  - Below, Hope higher: failure with Hope (gain Hope); Fear higher: failure
    with Fear.
  - Matching dice: **critical success** (success, gain Hope, clear 1 Stress;
    attacks add max damage). Counts as "with Hope".
- **Reaction roll:** same dice, no Hope or Fear generated.
- **Advantage / disadvantage:** +/− one d6; never more than one; they cancel.
- **Experience:** spend 1 Hope to add an Experience's modifier.
- Help an Ally (spend Hope, roll your own advantage d6), Tag Team (3 Hope,
  once per session).
- GM's Fear: up to 12, carries between sessions (GM-side, not on the sheet).

## 3. Character creation (pp. 4–6)

1. Class and subclass (13 classes, 2 subclasses each; foundation card).
2. Heritage: ancestry (24; mixed ancestry = top feature of one + bottom of
   another) and community (15). Optional transformation if the GM allows.
3. Traits: +2, +1, +1, +0, +0, −1.
4. Level 1, Evasion and HP from the class, 6 Stress, 2 Hope.
5. Equipment: Tier 1 weapons (two-handed primary, or one-handed primary +
   secondary), Tier 1 armor; torch, rope, basic supplies, a handful of gold,
   a Minor Health or Stamina Potion, a class item.
6. Background questions (from the class guide), description prompts.
7. Two Experiences at +2 (word or phrase; not too broad, no powers).
8. Two level-1 domain cards from the class's two domains.
9. Connections with the other PCs.

## 5. Domains and cards (pp. 7–8, 206+)

- Ten domains: Arcana, Blade, Bone, Codex, Dread (H&F), Grace, Midnight,
  Sage, Splendor, Valor. 21 cards each (three at level 1, two per level
  2–10): 210 cards, parsed from the appendix.
- Card: level, domain, type (ability, spell, grimoire), Recall Cost (Stress),
  feature text.
- **Loadout** of up to 5 active cards; the rest in the **vault**. Moving a
  vault card into the loadout costs its Recall Cost in Stress, except at the
  start of a rest. Subclass, ancestry, community and transformation cards
  don't count.

## 6. Leveling up (pp. 53–54)

- 10 levels in 4 tiers (1 · 2–4 · 5–7 · 8–10), levelled by GM milestone.
- Tier achievements at 2, 5, 8: new Experience +2, Proficiency +1 (5 and 8
  also clear marked traits).
- Two advancements per level from the tier's options: +1 to two unmarked
  traits, +1 HP slot, +1 Stress slot, +1 to two Experiences, an extra domain
  card, +1 Evasion, the next subclass card, +1 Proficiency (costs both),
  multiclass from level 5 (costs both).
- Thresholds +1 every level; one new domain card at or below the level
  (and an optional swap).

## 7. Rest, death, conditions (pp. 50–53)

- Short and long rests with downtime moves (clear HP, clear Stress, repair
  armor, prepare, work on a project…).
- Falling at the last HP: a death move (Blaze of Glory, Avoid Death, Risk It
  All).

- Plain `pdftotext` mixes the two columns: neighbouring ancestries and

  (`-layout` with column splitting, or per-column cropping) and a check
